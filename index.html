About

I'm a machine learning practitioner. My main expertise is in modeling dynamic environments, such as search and recommendation systems, and in multilingual, multimodal (text, image and audio) content understanding at scale.

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at Arena, working on machine learning research. Before that I spent five years at Coupang as the overall tech lead for search candidate generation and ranking, where I also led the ML strategy for search and recommendations. Before Coupang I spent eight years at Google, working on ads, YouTube and large multimodal models.

I've also been teaching since 2006, from competitive programming for high schoolers to data science at UC Berkeley Extension. I speak Mandarin, English and Cantonese.
